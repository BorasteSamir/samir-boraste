Mission Plan: Station S03

Primary Dispatch Target: Station S03 (Valley Library)

Target Subsystem: Digital Air Temperature Probe & Sensor Bus
URGENT FIELD DISPATCH

Why S03 and NOT the Second-Best Choice?

Every other station's anomaly is mathematically deterministic and already resolved: S06 was a fixed clock shift (+6h), S07 was a known unit scale (3.6×), S02 was a static board offset (-6.66 hPa), and S04/S08 issues are already past.

S03 is the only station with an ACTIVE, RECURRING HARDWARE FAILURE. Its digital temperature probe suffered -40°C open-circuit disconnections and +85°C reset defaults on Mar 05, Mar 12, Apr 02, and Apr 08, plus an 80-hour total freeze. Software cannot invent missing weather data. If not physically repaired, S03 will continue losing spring data.

What to Check FIRST (Minute 0–10):
Inspect the screw terminals inside the logger enclosure and radiation shield strain relief. A loose screw or corroded ground on a 1-wire digital bus produces intermittent disconnects (-40°C) and reset register states (+85°C).

1-Hour Field Protocol for the Volunteer

  1. Minute 00–10: Terminal Inspection: Open enclosure, inspect probe wiring to terminal block. Check for loose copper strands, moisture ingress, or oxidized contacts. Gently tug each wire.
  2. Minute 10–25: Sensor Replacement: Swap the Dallas DS18B20 temperature probe with the pre-tested spare from the spares kit. Tighten screw terminals firmly.
  3. Minute 25–35: Radiation Shield Check: Check the louvers of the radiation shield for spider webs, wasps' nests, or debris that might trap heat or moisture against the probe. Clean with brush.
  4. Minute 35–50: Real-Time Diagnostic: Power on logger display. Confirm live temperature updates every 10 seconds and tracks ambient temperature (compare against portable handheld thermometer).
  5. Minute 50–60: Close & Sign-off: Reseal weatherproof grommet with silicone grease, verify logger status LED blinks green, log replacement in physical notebook.
Equipment to bring: Small flathead terminal screwdriver, spare DS18B20 digital probe, handheld reference thermometer, lint-free cloth, desiccant pouch.
